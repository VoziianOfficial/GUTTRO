Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how GUTTRO may collect, use, and protect information submitted through this independent gutter provider comparison platform.

Last updated: June 2026

1. Overview

GUTTRO is an independent gutter provider comparison platform. The website helps homeowners explore gutter service categories, request provider options, and compare information related to independent local companies.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected when visitors use the website, submit a request, contact GUTTRO, or interact with website features.

GUTTRO does not perform gutter cleaning, repair, installation, or gutter guard work directly. Providers are independent, and homeowners should verify license, insurance, terms, and suitability before hiring any company.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect information you provide directly and information collected automatically through standard website technologies.

Information you provide

  • Full name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, and service interest.
  • Messages or project details submitted through the contact form.
  • Information related to gutter cleaning, repair, installation, gutter guards, or drainage concerns.
  • Communications sent by email, phone, or website form.

Information collected automatically

  • Browser type, device type, operating system, and approximate technical information.
  • Pages visited, referring URLs, interaction events, and general site usage patterns.
  • Cookie or localStorage choices, such as cookie banner accept or decline status.

3. How we may use information

Information may be used to operate the website, respond to requests, improve the user experience, and help organize gutter provider comparison paths.

  • To respond to contact form submissions and service comparison requests.
  • To organize requests by gutter service category, location, and project details.
  • To help connect homeowners with independent provider options where available.
  • To improve website performance, content, navigation, security, and user experience.
  • To maintain records, comply with legal obligations, and protect against misuse.

4. How information may be shared

Submitted information may be shared when needed to support the gutter provider comparison process, operate the website, or comply with applicable obligations.

  • With independent service providers or comparison partners when relevant to a submitted request.
  • With vendors that help operate the website, process communications, host content, or support analytics.
  • When required by law, legal process, security needs, or to protect rights and safety.
  • In connection with a business transfer, restructuring, or similar operational event.

GUTTRO does not claim that any provider is suitable for a specific project. Homeowners are responsible for reviewing and verifying each provider before hiring.

5. Your choices

You may choose not to submit personal information through the website. Some features, such as requesting provider options, may not be available without certain details.

  • You may contact GUTTRO to request access, correction, or deletion of information where applicable.
  • You may manage cookies through your browser settings and the website cookie banner.
  • You may decline future communications by using available contact options or replying with your request.

Data security

GUTTRO uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect submitted information. However, no online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children’s privacy

The website is intended for adults and homeowners seeking gutter provider comparison information. It is not directed to children.
