2. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small files or pieces of data that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies can include localStorage, sessionStorage, pixels, tags, and scripts.

These technologies may help the website remember settings, support security, measure usage, and improve how pages and forms work.
